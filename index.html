Aerodynamic Variable Glossary

Standard engineering definitions for essential atmospheric, fluid, and flight variables.

α

Angle of Attack (AoA)

The geometric angle between the airfoil chord line and the relative wind vector. Governs aerodynamic force generation until reaching the critical stall angle.

Symbol: α | Unit: Degrees (°)
v

True Airspeed (TAS)

The speed of the aircraft relative to the undisturbed surrounding air mass. Dynamic pressure scales quadratically with airspeed (v²).

Symbol: v | Unit: m/s or Knots
ρ

Air Density (Rho)

Mass per unit volume of air. Decreases with altitude and temperature, directly scaling total lift and drag forces produced.

Symbol: ρ | Unit: kg/m³
S

Wing Reference Area

The projected planform area of the wing surface. Multiplied by dynamic pressure to convert dimensionless coefficients into physical force vectors.

Symbol: S | Unit: Square Meters (m²)
CL

Lift Coefficient

Dimensionless ratio quantifying the lift generated by an airfoil shape relative to dynamic pressure and wing area.

Symbol: CL | Unit: Dimensionless
CD

Drag Coefficient

Dimensionless parameter representing total resistance, combining skin friction, pressure parasite drag, and lift-induced drag (CDi).

Symbol: CD | Unit: Dimensionless
