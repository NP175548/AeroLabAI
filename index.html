Core Aerodynamic Physics

The simulation calculates real-time 2D forces by summing lift, drag, thrust, and gravitational weight vectors relative to the flight path angle.

Lift Equation

$$L = \frac{1}{2} (C_{L} + C_{L,\text{flaps}}) \rho v^2 S$$

Drag Equation

$$D = \frac{1}{2} (C_{D} + C_{D,\text{flaps}}) \rho v^2 S$$

Aircraft Weight

$$W = m \cdot g$$

Net Thrust Force

$$T = T_{\text{max}} \cdot \text{Throttle}\%$$

Variable & Visual Legend

Symbol / Indicator Definition Aerodynamic Effect
$L$ (Lift) Lift Force Acts perpendicular to airflow vector. Increased by pitch angle and flap deployment.
$D$ (Drag) Aerodynamic Drag Resists forward movement along velocity vector. Increases with AoA, speed, and flaps.
$T$ (Thrust) Forward Thrust Engine force controlled directly via the throttle slider to overcome drag and sustain airspeed.
$W$ / $m$ (Weight/Mass) Mass Force ($m \cdot g$) Higher weight demands more thrust and higher lift coefficients to prevent sinking.
$\alpha$ (AoA) Angle of Attack ($\theta - \gamma$) Difference between pitch angle ($\theta$) and flight path ($\gamma$). Exceeding 15° triggers aerodynamic stall.
Blue Line Wing Chord Reference Indicates physical pitch orientation set by the pitch angle slider.
Cyan Streams Relative Airflow Particles Dynamic stream lines matching visual air speed and direction in the tunnel.
