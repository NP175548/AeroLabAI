Aerodynamic Principles

Understanding how fluid motion creates aerodynamic forces.

1. Bernoulli's Principle

As air velocity increases over the curved upper surface of an airfoil, static pressure decreases relative to the flatter lower surface, creating an upward pressure differential.

P + ½ · ρ · v² = Constant

2. Newton's Third Law

The wing's Angle of Attack deflects oncoming air downward (downwash). By Newton's Third Law, deflecting air downward creates an equal and opposite upward reaction force on the wing.

Flift = - (Δpair / Δt)

3. Angle of Attack & Stall

Increasing the Angle of Attack increases lift up to a critical point (αcrit ≈ 15°). Beyond this threshold, smooth airflow detaches from the top surface, resulting in a stall.

CD,induced = CL² / (π · AR · e)

4. Aspect Ratio & Design

High Aspect Ratio wings (gliders) minimize induced drag for high endurance flight. Low Aspect Ratio wings (fighters) offer structural strength and fast roll rates for agility.

Aspect Ratio (AR) = b² / S
