Core Aerodynamic Physics

The simulation uses full 2D force summation accounting for aircraft weight, lift, drag, and true angle of attack relative to the flight path vector.

Lift Equation

$$L = \frac{1}{2} C_L \cdot \rho \cdot v^2 \cdot S$$

Drag Equation

$$D = \frac{1}{2} C_D \cdot \rho \cdot v^2 \cdot S$$

Weight & Gravity Force

$$W = m \cdot g$$

Net Vertical Acceleration

$$a_y = \frac{L \cdot \cos(\gamma) - D \cdot \sin(\gamma) - W}{m}$$

Variable & Visual Legend

Symbol / Line Definition Physical Function
$L$ (Lift) Lift Force Acts perpendicular to the relative airflow vector to push aircraft upward.
$D$ (Drag) Aerodynamic Drag Acts parallel and opposite to velocity vector, resisting movement.
$W$ / $m$ (Weight/Mass) Aircraft Weight Heavier aircraft require higher speed ($v$) or pitch angle to maintain altitude.
$\alpha$ (AoA) Angle of Attack Difference between Pitch Angle ($\theta$) and Flight Path Angle ($\gamma$). Exceeding 15° causes stall.
Blue Line Chord Reference Line Indicates physical pitch orientation of the wing relative to horizontal plane.
