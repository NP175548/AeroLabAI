Aerodynamic Principles

Theory & Equations

Bernoulli's Principle

Fluid velocity increase over the curved upper surface of an airfoil reduces static local pressure relative to the lower surface.

P + ½ ρ v² = Constant

Newton's Third Law

Deflection of passing air downward (downwash) creates an equal and opposite reaction force pushing the wing upward.

F = Δp / Δt

Angle of Attack & Stall

Beyond critical angle (αcrit ≈ 15°), airflow detaches from the upper wing surface, collapsing lift.

α > αcrit ⇒ Boundary Layer Separation

The Lift Equation

Total lift is proportional to dynamic pressure (½ ρ V²), wing reference surface area (S), and lift coefficient (CL).

L = ½ · ρ · V² · S · CL
